First introduced in April 2023, SenseNova encompasses large language models , text-to-image generators, multimodal reasoning systems, spatial intelligence models, and a unified understanding-and-generation architecture. The platform has evolved through multiple major generations, from its initial launch as a suite of separate task-specific models to the SenseNova V6 flagship released in April 2025, a native Mixture-of-Experts multimodal model with over six hundred billion parameters . SenseNova also includes specialized sub-brands such as SenseChat for conversational AI, SenseMirage for image generation, and SenseAvatar for digital human creation. In April 2026, SenseTime open-sourced SenseNova U1, a unified multimodal model built on the NEO-unify architecture that eliminates the need for separate visual encoders and variational autoencoders, enabling direct pixel-space image generation alongside text understanding and reasoning within a single model. SenseNova U1 is notable for its ability to produce complex infographics, sequential image-text compositions, and other visually dense content at commercial-grade quality, making it the first open-source model to unify continuous image-text creation in a single architecture. Background and development SenseTime was co-founded in October 2014 by Tang Xiao'ou, a professor in the Department of Information Engineering at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, and Xu Li, among other researchers drawn from CUHK's Multimedia Laboratory. The company initially specialized in computer vision and deep learning technologies, including facial recognition, object detection, and optical character recognition. In June 2014, the founding team's DeepID series face recognition algorithm achieved an accuracy rate of 98.52 percent, surpassing Facebook's contemporaneous DeepFace algorithm and marking the first time that machine recognition accuracy exceeded human-level performance on that benchmark. SenseTime's early research output was prolific, with nine papers accepted at the 2015 Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition and sixteen papers accepted at CVPR 2016, alongside a first-place finish in the ImageNet competition for object detection, video object detection, and scene analysis. SenseTime listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in December 2021, completing an approximately 851 million dollar stock offering despite United States Treasury Department sanctions imposed that same month. The sanctions barred American investors from owning or trading SenseTime stock, citing the role that SenseTime's facial recognition technology allegedly played in surveillance of Uyghur and other Muslim minorities in Xinjiang. The company's shares debuted at a valuation of approximately seventeen billion dollars. Tang Xiao'ou died unexpectedly in December 2023 at the age of 55, causing SenseTime's shares to plunge as much as eighteen percent, though the company stated that the loss would have no material operational impact. The pivot toward generative AI began in earnest in 2023. On April 10, 2023, SenseTime hosted a Technology Exchange Day at which chairman and CEO Xu Li introduced the "Ririxin SenseNova" large model system as part of the company's strategic plan for advancing artificial general intelligence development through the combination of foundation models and large-scale computing. This initial release comprised SenseChat, a large language model with hundreds of billions of parameters trained with particular attention to Chinese-language contexts; SenseMirage, a text-to-image creation platform capable of generating 6K ultra-high-definition images with realistic lighting and diverse styles; and SenseAvatar, a digital human video generation platform. By August 2023, SenseTime had already upgraded the SenseNova foundation model sets, adding capabilities in areas such as multi-turn dialogue, code generation, and cross-document knowledge extraction. SenseNova 4.0 followed on February 6, 2024, delivering what the company described as a multi-dimensional upgrade across knowledge coverage, reasoning, long text comprehension, numerical inference, code generation, and multimodal interactions. The accompanying SenseChat V4 performed competitively with GPT-4 while surpassing GPT-3 in overall benchmarks, and supported context windows of four thousand, thirty-two thousand, and one hundred twenty-eight thousand tokens. Specialized variants included SenseChat-DataAnalysis V4 for office and business analytics, SenseChat-Medical V4 for healthcare applications, and SenseChat-Vision V4 for autonomous driving and industrial scenarios. SenseMirage V4 increased its parameters to ten billion and could produce cinematic-grade poster images, while SenseMirage-Turbo V4 achieved inference times ten times faster than the base V4 version. SenseNova 5.0 launched at SenseTime's Tech Day in Shanghai on April 23, 2024. Adopting a Mixture-of-Experts architecture with a three-tier Knowledge, Reasoning, and Execution design, the model was trained on over ten terabytes of tokens including substantial synthetic data and achieved an effective context window of approximately two hundred thousand tokens during inference. SenseNova 5.0 claimed first place on the MMBench multimodal aggregate benchmark and posted high scores on MathVista, AI2D, and ChartQA. The announcement caused SenseTime's stock price to surge approximately thirty-six percent. Edge-side deployment capabilities included language model inference at 18.3 words per second on mid-range platforms and 78.3 words per second on flagship platforms, while the accompanying LDM-AI image diffusion technology achieved sub-1.5-second inference on mainstream platforms with support for twelve-megapixel and higher resolution output. SenseNova 5.5 was unveiled at the 2024 World Artificial Intelligence Conference in Shanghai in early July 2024. This release introduced SenseNova 5o, which SenseTime described as China's first real-time multimodal model, offering streaming interaction capabilities analogous to those of GPT-4o . SenseNova 5o gathered and processed data across audio, text, image, and video modalities simultaneously, enabling conversational interactions with response patterns comparable to real-time human dialogue. SenseTime claimed that SenseNova 5.5 outperformed GPT-4o in five out of eight key performance metrics, with a thirty percent overall improvement over version 5.0, including enhanced mathematical reasoning, English proficiency, and instruction-following. The company also launched the "Project $0 Go" scheme at this time, offering enterprise users migrating from OpenAI's platform a free onboarding package including fifty million tokens and API migration consulting. SenseNova V6 arrived at SenseTime's April 2025 Tech Day, representing the most significant architectural leap in the platform's history. As a native Mixture-of-Experts multimodal foundation model with over six hundred billion parameters, V6 was designed from the ground up to handle both text and multimodal tasks within a single model rather than relying on separate specialized components. The model was trained on over two hundred billion tokens of high-quality multimodal long chain-of-thought data, with maximum reasoning sequences reaching sixty-four thousand tokens. SenseTime positioned V6 as having multimodal reasoning capabilities ranked first in China when benchmarked against GPT-o1, with data analysis performance surpassing that of GPT-4o. V6 also became the first Chinese large model capable of in-depth analysis of ten-minute mid-to-long-form videos, compressing footage into sixteen thousand tokens while preserving key semantic content. The accompanying SenseNova V6 Omni variant was deployed commercially across multiple industries. Architecture The SenseNova family encompasses several distinct architectural paradigms that have evolved across its generations. The earliest models in the SenseNova lineup used dense transformer architectures trained for specific tasks, with SenseChat handling natural language processing, SenseMirage performing text-to-image generation via diffusion techniques, and SenseAvatar managing digital human synthesis. Beginning with SenseNova 5.0, SenseTime adopted a Mixture-of-Experts approach organized around what it termed a Knowledge, Reasoning, and Execution three-tier architecture. In this design, the Knowledge layer handles information retrieval and factual grounding, the Reasoning layer performs logical inference and chain-of-thought processing, and the Execution layer translates model outputs into actionable results across modalities. This layered structure allowed SenseNova 5.0 to achieve an effective context window of approximately two hundred thousand tokens while maintaining efficient inference characteristics. SenseNova V6, the flagship model as of April 2025, is a native Mixture-of-Experts multimodal foundation model. Unlike earlier versions that relied on separate model components for different modalities stitched together at the application layer, V6 processes text, images, audio, and video within a unified parameter space. The model contains over six hundred billion total parameters, though the MoE architecture means that only a fraction of these parameters are active for any given input, allowing for more efficient inference than a comparably sized dense model would provide. V6's multimodal long chain-of-thought capability extends to sixty-four thousand tokens, enabling extended reasoning across visual and textual inputs. For video understanding, V6 employs a compression mechanism that reduces ten minutes of video footage to approximately sixteen thousand tokens while preserving semantically important content, allowing the model to perform in-depth video analysis without exceeding practical context limits. The most architecturally distinctive member of the SenseNova family is SenseNova U1, introduced in April 2026 and built on the NEO-unify architecture. NEO-unify represents a fundamental departure from conventional multimodal model design by eliminating both the visual encoder and the variational autoencoder that are standard components in most vision-language and image generation models. In traditional architectures, images are first processed through a visual encoder such as a Vision Transformer to produce latent representations, and generated images are decoded through a VAE from latent space back to pixel space. NEO-unify instead operates directly in pixel space using pixel-space flow matching for visual generation, processing images and text within a shared representation space. This design reduces information loss during the encoding and decoding stages and produces better consistency when creating dense visual content such as infographics, guides, posters, comics, and interleaved image-text workflows. The U1 Lite series was released in two configurations, the SenseNova U1-8B-MoT built on a dense eight-billion-parameter backbone and the SenseNova U1-A3B-MoT built on a Mixture-of-Experts backbone with thirty billion total parameters and approximately three billion active parameters per input. SenseNova-SI, the spatial intelligence variant released in December 2025, employs a different approach focused on enabling autonomous systems to perceive distance, position, and movement direction. Available in two-billion and eight-billion parameter configurations, the SI models were trained using what SenseTime described as a systematic methodology that demonstrated the scaling law in the field of spatial intelligence for the first time, showing that enhanced data quality and quantity correlate with improved performance across six core dimensions including metric measurement, mental reconstruction, spatial relationships, perspective-taking, deformation and assembling, and comprehensive spatial reasoning. Training data and methodology SenseTime's approach to training the SenseNova models has evolved substantially across generations, reflecting broader trends in the AI industry toward larger datasets, synthetic data augmentation, and specialized training regimes for different capabilities. The original SenseChat model launched in April 2023 was described as a large language model with hundreds of billions of parameters, trained with particular attention to Chinese-language data to ensure strong comprehension and generation of Chinese texts. Specific details about the training corpus for the initial versions were not publicly disclosed, though the model's demonstrated capabilities in multi-turn Chinese dialogue and cross-document Chinese knowledge extraction suggest a training set heavily weighted toward Chinese-language internet, academic, and enterprise data. SenseNova 5.0, launched in April 2024, was trained on over ten terabytes of tokens, a significant increase that included a substantial volume of synthetic data. The use of synthetic training data reflected SenseTime's strategy of generating high-quality training examples through existing models to improve performance on specific tasks such as mathematical reasoning and code generation. The ten-terabyte figure placed SenseNova 5.0's training corpus in a comparable range to other major foundation models of its generation, though SenseTime did not provide a detailed breakdown of source categories. SenseNova 5.5, released in July 2024, expanded the training methodology by incorporating synthetically generated reasoning chain data. This approach involved using earlier models to produce step-by-step reasoning traces for complex problems, which were then used to train the next generation. The inclusion of reasoning chain data contributed to the thirty percent overall performance improvement that SenseTime reported over version 5.0, particularly in mathematical reasoning and instruction-following benchmarks. The training methodology for SenseNova V6 represented the most substantial investment in multimodal data preparation. SenseTime reported training the model on over two hundred billion tokens of high-quality multimodal long chain-of-thought data, a twenty-fold increase over the text-focused ten-terabyte corpus of version 5.0. This multimodal corpus included paired text-image data, video transcripts with temporal annotations, and extended reasoning sequences that spanned multiple modalities. The multimodal long chain-of-thought data was specifically curated to enable V6's extended reasoning capability, with individual training sequences reaching up to sixty-four thousand tokens in length. SenseTime claimed that V6's multimodal training efficiency matched that of language-only models, which would represent a notable achievement given that multimodal training typically incurs higher computational costs per token due to the heterogeneity of visual and textual data. SenseNova-SI's training for spatial intelligence tasks employed what SenseTime described as a systematic methodology focused on high-quality, large-scale training data covering six core spatial dimensions. The research team demonstrated scaling laws in spatial intelligence, showing measurable performance improvements correlated with increases in both data volume and quality. The SenseNova-SI-8M dataset, comprising eight million samples and released alongside the model weights on Hugging Face , represents one of the largest publicly available spatial intelligence training sets. For SenseNova U1, the training methodology was shaped by the NEO-unify architecture's unique requirements. Because the model operates directly in pixel space without a VAE or visual encoder, the training process needed to jointly optimize for text understanding, visual comprehension, reasoning, and pixel-level image generation within a single loss function . The training approach drew on SenseTime's accumulated experience across all prior SenseNova generations, combining language pretraining, multimodal alignment, reasoning chain training, and pixel-space flow matching into a unified training pipeline. All SenseNova models were trained on SenseTime's proprietary SenseCore AI infrastructure, which includes the company's Shanghai-based Artificial Intelligence Data Center covering 130,000 square meters and housing 5,000 computer racks, with a total computing capacity exceeding 4.91 exaFLOPS across 23 AI supercomputing clusters worldwide. SenseTime also developed its own deep learning training framework, SenseParrots, and a deep learning inference engine, SensePPL, which together form the basis of the company's model training and deployment pipeline. Capabilities and performance The SenseNova model family spans a broad range of capabilities that have expanded with each generation. At its core, SenseNova provides general-purpose language understanding and generation, multimodal reasoning across text, images, audio, and video, text-to-image generation, real-time conversational interaction, spatial intelligence, and unified understanding-generation processing. In language tasks, SenseNova V6 performs on par with leading international models such as GPT-4.5 and Gemini 2.0 Pro , according to SenseTime's reported benchmarks. The model's data analysis capabilities were reported to outperform GPT-4o, while its multimodal deep reasoning capabilities were ranked first among Chinese models when benchmarked against GPT-o1. SenseChat V4, the conversational interface built on SenseNova 4.0, achieved an 85.71 percent accuracy rate in data analysis scenarios comprising more than one thousand questions, surpassing GPT-4 on that specific evaluation. SenseNova 5.0 claimed first place on the MMBench multimodal aggregate benchmark and achieved strong scores on mathematical benchmarks including MathVista, as well as document understanding benchmarks such as AI2D and ChartQA. Video understanding represents one of SenseNova V6's most distinctive capabilities. The model can perform in-depth analysis of videos up to ten minutes in length, making it the first Chinese large model to support mid-to-long-form video comprehension at that scale. The system compresses video footage into approximately sixteen thousand tokens while preserving semantically important content, enabling detailed temporal reasoning about events, actions, and narrative structure within videos. SenseTime benchmarked this capability against Gemini 2.5 Turbo and reported V6 to be among the strongest models in its class for video analysis. SenseNova 5o, introduced as part of the 5.5 release, provides real-time multimodal streaming interaction. The model simultaneously processes audio, text, image, and video inputs and generates responses with latency characteristics suitable for real-time conversational use. This capability enables applications such as real-time speech recognition, simultaneous translation, and interactive visual analysis, with response patterns that SenseTime described as comparable to natural human conversation. In image generation, the SenseNova platform has progressed from SenseMirage's initial 6K ultra-high-definition output in 2023 through the ten-billion-parameter SenseMirage V4 in early 2024 to the architecturally novel SenseNova U1 in 2026. SenseMirage V4 could produce cinematic-grade poster images, and its Turbo variant achieved inference times ten times faster than the base version. The edge-side LDM-AI image diffusion technology in SenseNova 5.0 achieved sub-1.5-second inference on mainstream platforms with support for twelve-megapixel and higher resolution output, including proportional, free-form, and rotational image expansion. SenseNova U1 advanced the state of the art by generating images directly in pixel space using flow matching rather than latent diffusion, producing commercial-grade output quality comparable to Seedream 4.5 and Qwen-Image 2.0 Pro while achieving approximately twice the inference speed of those systems for complex infographic generation. Infographic and complex layout generation is a particular strength of SenseNova U1, which SenseTime positioned as the first model to deliver continuous image-text creative generation within a unified architecture. The model demonstrates strong control over layout coherence and text rendering accuracy in infographic production, a task that has historically been challenging for both open-source and commercial models due to the requirement for precise spatial positioning of text elements alongside graphical content. This capability extends to sequential images, comics, posters, guides, and other formats that interleave text and visual elements. SenseNova-SI's spatial intelligence capabilities represent a specialized branch of the platform focused on three-dimensional spatial understanding. The eight-billion-parameter SenseNova-SI model achieved an average score of 60.0 points across five major spatial intelligence benchmarks, compared to 52.1 points for GPT-5 and 56.2 points for Gemini-3-Pro-Preview. On the MindCube benchmark developed by NYU and Stanford, SenseNova-SI-8B scored 85.6 percent compared to GPT-5's 34.4 percent, while on the VSI-Bench it achieved 68.7 percent compared to GPT-5's 55.0 percent. These benchmarks evaluate capabilities across metric measurement, mental reconstruction, spatial relationships, perspective-taking, deformation and assembling, and comprehensive spatial reasoning. Deployment and availability SenseNova models are available through multiple deployment channels spanning cloud APIs, edge devices, enterprise on-premises installations, and open-source releases. SenseTime's primary cloud access point is SenseChat, available at chat.sensetime.com, which provides consumer and developer access to the latest SenseNova models. For enterprise customers, SenseTime offers API access through the SenseChat Function Call and Assistants API, which supports tool-calling, network search, code interpretation, image and text question-answering, and text-to-image generation. Since the launch of SenseNova, the platform has attracted over three thousand enterprise users across industries including internet services, gaming, culture and tourism, education, healthcare, finance, and software development. Edge deployment has been a consistent focus of SenseTime's strategy, reflecting the company's "Cloud-to-Edge" product philosophy. SenseNova 5.0 introduced edge-optimized model variants capable of running on consumer-grade hardware, with language model inference reaching 78.3 words per second on flagship mobile platforms and image generation completing in under 1.5 seconds. SenseNova 5.5 further reduced edge deployment costs, with SenseTime claiming annual per-device costs as low as 9.90 Chinese yuan. These edge capabilities were integrated into products from partners including Kingsoft Office's WPS 365 productivity suite, Haitong Securities' financial analysis tools, and Xiaomi's SU7 electric vehicle. SenseTime has committed significant resources to open-source releases in the SenseNova ecosystem. SenseNova-SI, the spatial intelligence variant, was open-sourced in December 2025 with model weights in two-billion and eight-billion parameter configurations available on Hugging Face, along with the full eight-million-sample SenseNova-SI-8M training dataset, model code on GitHub under the OpenSenseNova organization, and the EASI Spatial Intelligence Evaluation Platform and Leaderboard. A technical report was published on arXiv. SenseNova U1 Lite was open-sourced on April 29, 2026, with the eight-billion-parameter dense variant and the thirty-billion-parameter MoE variant made available on both GitHub and Hugging Face. The accompanying research paper describing the NEO-unify architecture was published on arXiv in May 2026. SenseNova V6 Omni, the commercially deployed real-time interactive variant of V6, has been made available across multiple industries through SenseTime's enterprise sales channels. At the V6 launch event, SenseTime announced a one hundred million yuan subsidy program for embodied intelligence and AI-generated content implementation, aimed at accelerating enterprise adoption of the platform. The company has also pursued international deployment, though its expansion outside China has been complicated by the ongoing United States sanctions that restrict American investment in SenseTime. All model training and inference infrastructure runs on SenseTime's proprietary SenseCore AI Cloud, which provides an out-of-the-box industrial-grade AI toolchain covering the AI native infrastructure layer, the deep learning platform layer, and the algorithm model layer. SenseCore can scale seamlessly from one to ten thousand GPUs and is anchored by the company's Shanghai Lingang Artificial Intelligence Data Center, which at its launch was described as the largest AI computing center in Asia, covering 130,000 square meters with 5,000 computer racks and a computing capacity of 3,740 petaflops. SenseTime's total computing capacity across its 23 worldwide supercomputing clusters exceeds 4.91 exaFLOPS. SenseNova version history The SenseNova platform has undergone rapid iteration since its initial launch, with major releases approximately every six to twelve months and significant capability expansions at each step. The original SenseNova system, launched on April 10, 2023, comprised three separate components: SenseChat for natural language processing, SenseMirage for text-to-image generation, and SenseAvatar for digital human video creation. SenseChat was built as a dense large language model with hundreds of billions of parameters, while SenseMirage supported 6K ultra-high-definition image generation. The August 2023 upgrade added improved multi-turn dialogue capabilities, code generation, and cross-document knowledge extraction. SenseNova 4.0, released on February 6, 2024, introduced the expanded specialized model variants including SenseChat-DataAnalysis V4, SenseChat-Medical V4, SenseChat-Vision V4, and the ten-billion-parameter SenseMirage V4. This version also launched the SenseChat Function Call and Assistants API and the Office Raccoon data analysis tool. SenseNova 5.0, released on April 23, 2024, marked the transition to the Mixture-of-Experts architecture with the Knowledge, Reasoning, and Execution three-tier design. Trained on over ten terabytes of tokens, it achieved a two-hundred-thousand-token context window and introduced edge-side deployment capabilities. The stock price surge of approximately thirty-six percent following the announcement reflected market enthusiasm for the platform's competitive positioning against GPT-4 Turbo. SenseNova 5.5, unveiled at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference in early July 2024, introduced SenseNova 5o, China's first real-time multimodal model with streaming interaction capabilities. SenseTime reported a thirty percent overall performance improvement over version 5.0, with gains concentrated in mathematical reasoning, English proficiency, and instruction-following. SenseNova V6, launched in April 2025, represented the largest architectural leap with its native MoE multimodal design containing over six hundred billion parameters. Trained on over two hundred billion multimodal chain-of-thought tokens, V6 introduced ten-minute video analysis, sixty-four-thousand-token reasoning chains, and claimed the lowest multimodal training and inference costs in the industry. SenseNova-SI, released in December 2025, specialized in spatial intelligence and was fully open-sourced with model weights, training data, and evaluation tools. SenseNova U1, released on April 29, 2026, introduced the NEO-unify architecture for unified multimodal understanding and generation, becoming the first open-source model to achieve continuous image-text creative generation within a single architecture. The research paper introducing the U1 architecture appeared as a CVPR 2026 publication. Significance and impact SenseNova occupies a distinctive position in the global AI landscape as one of the most comprehensive model platform efforts to emerge from China's AI industry. While Chinese companies such as DeepSeek , Kimi , Alibaba's Qwen , and Baidu's Ernie have each developed significant foundation models, SenseNova's breadth across language, vision, multimodal reasoning, spatial intelligence, image generation, and real-time interaction within a single unified brand is relatively unusual. This breadth reflects SenseTime's origins as a computer vision company that expanded into language models rather than the reverse path taken by most large language model developers. The platform's development trajectory has mirrored and at times anticipated broader industry trends. SenseNova's adoption of Mixture-of-Experts architecture in version 5.0 in April 2024 aligned with a wider industry shift toward MoE designs that would later be employed by models such as GPT-4o and Gemini 2.0 . The introduction of real-time multimodal streaming in SenseNova 5o in July 2024 came shortly after OpenAI demonstrated similar capabilities with GPT-4o, suggesting parallel development timelines. SenseNova V6's emphasis on multimodal chain-of-thought reasoning reflected the reasoning-focused direction that would become dominant across the industry in 2025. SenseNova U1's NEO-unify architecture represents a potentially significant contribution to the field of multimodal AI. By eliminating the visual encoder and variational autoencoder that are standard in virtually all other vision-language and image generation models, NEO-unify proposes an alternative architectural paradigm in which images and text share the same representation space from the ground up. If the approach proves scalable beyond the current Lite configurations, it could influence how future unified multimodal models are designed across the industry. The open-sourcing of U1 under the OpenSenseNova GitHub organization, along with the publication of the underlying research at CVPR 2026, ensures that the architectural innovations are available for the broader research community to evaluate and build upon. SenseNova-SI's demonstration of scaling laws in spatial intelligence and its strong benchmark performance against GPT-5 and Gemini-3-Pro have implications for the field of embodied AI and robotics. The open-sourcing of the eight-million-sample training dataset alongside the model weights provides the research community with resources that had previously been unavailable for spatial intelligence research at this scale. From a commercial perspective, SenseNova has been central to SenseTime's transformation from a computer vision company under United States sanctions pressure into a generative AI platform business. Generative AI revenue, driven primarily by SenseNova-based products, grew 103.1 percent year-on-year in 2024 to exceed 2.4 billion yuan, accounting for approximately sixty-four percent of SenseTime's total revenue. The company's total revenue for 2024 reached 3.77 billion yuan, though SenseTime continued to report a net loss of approximately 4.3 billion yuan. In the first half of 2025, generative AI revenue surged 72.7 percent year-on-year to reach 18.155 billion yuan, contributing 77 percent of total group revenue, while the adjusted net loss was halved to 1.16 billion yuan. This financial trajectory suggests that SenseNova has become the primary driver of SenseTime's business model and its path toward eventual profitability. The platform's competitive significance within the Chinese AI ecosystem is amplified by SenseTime's infrastructure advantages. The SenseCore AI Cloud and the Shanghai Lingang AIDC provide SenseTime with one of the largest AI computing installations in Asia, supporting rapid model iteration and enabling the company to offer competitive pricing for both cloud API access and edge deployment. SenseTime's "Cloud-to-Edge" strategy, which offers enterprise model deployment at annual per-device costs as low as 9.90 yuan, has contributed to the platform's adoption by over three thousand enterprise customers. The one hundred million yuan subsidy program announced alongside SenseNova V6 further demonstrates SenseTime's commitment to building an ecosystem around the SenseNova platform.
