# SenseTime 2025 Annual Results News

Source: SenseTime Official News
URL: https://www.sensetime.com/en/news/51170581/
Published: 2026-03-25

## Headline
SenseTime Group Reports Record High Revenue of Over RMB 5 billion in 2025; Second Half EBITDA Turns Positive

## Key Financial Data
- Total revenue: rose 33% year-on-year to more than RMB 5 billion (record high, fastest growth in 3 years)
- Net loss: narrowed by 58.6% year-on-year
- Adjusted net loss: declined for a fourth consecutive half-year, with accelerating pace
- H2 2025 EBITDA: RMB 380 million, turning positive for the first time since listing
- Operating cash flow: positive net inflow in H2 2025, first such achievement since listing
- Trade receivables collection: RMB 4.87 billion (record high)
- CV 2.0: achieved profitability for the first time and generated positive cash flow for second consecutive year

## Product & Technology
- SenseNova V6.5 Pro ranked #1 in China on SuperCLUE December 2025 multimodal benchmark (score 75.35)
- NEO: new native multimodal model architecture, released and open-sourced in Dec 2025
- NEO achieves SOTA comparable to peers with only 1/10 training data and computing power
- SenseCore: total operational computing scale reached 40,400 PetaFLOPS (FP16)
- SenseCore supported nearly 1 million model R&D tasks in 2025
- Partnered with 10+ chip makers (Huawei Ascend, Hygon, Cambricon)
- Launched China's first overseas Chinese-led computing cluster in Saudi Arabia

## Strategy
- "1+X" strategy: core platform provides foundational capabilities, ecosystem partners scale in verticals
- CV market leadership: maintained #1 in China for 9 consecutive years
- Kapi product series: user base in tens of millions
- 2026 plan: new foundational model based on NEO architecture in Q2 2026
